Asteroid Miner Tycoon

Privacy Policy

By OpenEyesAI · Effective October 18, 2026

The short version

  • You never make an account, and we never ask for your name, email or anything else about you.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your own device. We have no servers and receive none of it.
  • The game shows ads. The ad companies listed below collect some information about your device to show and measure those ads.

What stays on your device

Your progress (money, upgrades, drones, outfits and so on) and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are stored in the app's own storage on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or choosing Reset all progress in its settings, deletes them.

Ads

The game is free and is paid for by ads: a banner in the shop, and short videos you can choose to watch for a reward. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may also show ads from these partner networks through its mediation service:

  • AppLovin
  • Unity Ads

To show and measure ads, these companies may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • information about your device, such as its model, operating system, language and screen size;
  • your IP address, and the approximate location that can be worked out from it;
  • which ads you see, tap or watch, and diagnostic information about how ads perform.

They use it to deliver ads, count views and taps, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show ads based on your interests. Each company handles this information under its own privacy policy:

  • Google: Privacy Policy, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AppLovin: Privacy Policy
  • Unity: Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads may use your information for personalised advertising, using Google's consent tool. You can change your answer at any time in the game's settings, under Ad privacy.
  • Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (on most Android phones: Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Reward videos are always optional. You can play the whole game without watching one.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files, precise location, or anything from your microphone or camera. The game asks only for internet access and use of the advertising ID (both for ads) and for vibration (for its haptic effects).

We don't sell your personal information. The ad partners above process information as described in their own policies.

Children

Asteroid Miner Tycoon is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age for consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Keeping and protecting information

We keep no information about you on any server. The ad partners keep the information they collect for as long as their own policies say.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
